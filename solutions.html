02 / HARDWARE-BASED ENCRYPTION

A small object.
A powerful
intention.

A dedicated microchip integrated into a USB device, conceived to initiate a computer-encryption process when connected.

The concept explores a direct connection between dedicated hardware and device protection, with an emphasis on making the initiation of encryption straightforward.

Compatibility, authorization, encryption scope, recovery procedures and supported operating systems must be defined and verified for the final product.
